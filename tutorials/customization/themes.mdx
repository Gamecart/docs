**Glass**, **Orbit**, and **Nexus** are the official storefront themes. **Glass** is the default.

## Switch the theme

1. Open **Your store > Customization**.
2. Click **Manage themes**. On Customization pages you can also click **Change theme** in the **Active theme** header.
3. Under **Default templates**, click **Use theme** on **Glass**, **Orbit**, or **Nexus**.

## Official themes

**Glass** is a dark glassmorphism theme.

**Orbit** is a dark storefront with a sidebar and a blurred header band.

Glass and Orbit don't list products on the home page.

**Nexus** is a dark storefront. It shows the full catalog on the home page, with one section per category.

## Theme options and visual identity

**Customization > Theme options** has settings for the active theme.

**Customization > Visual identity** is where you set colors and the logo. Both apply across themes.
