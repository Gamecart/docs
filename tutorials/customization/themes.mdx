Your storefront uses one official theme. To switch, select **Glass**, **Orbit**, or **Nexus** in the dashboard.

## Official themes

**Glass** is the default theme.

**Orbit** is an official theme.

**Nexus** shows the full catalog on the home page. Each category has its own section.
