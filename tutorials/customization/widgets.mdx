Open **Your store > Customization > Widgets**. The page is under **Active theme**.

Widgets are grouped into sections. Each section label shows how many widgets in that section are on, written as **N of M active**. Every card has a live preview, an on/off switch, and a gear icon. Click a card to configure that widget, then click **Save widget**.

Glass, Orbit, and Nexus can show every theme widget. The Discord card works with any theme. The page lists saved widgets the active theme can't show under **Unavailable in this theme**.

## Donations and sales

**Donation goal** shows progress toward the monthly goal.

**Top donors** lists the top supporters this month.

**Recent payments** lists the latest approved purchases.

**Donation goal** and **Top donors** reset on the 1st of each month at midnight in the store timezone. Set the timezone in **Settings > General**.

## Communication

**Announcement** is a notice bar at the top of the storefront. Wrap words in `[brackets]` to make them bold. You can add a button and a link.

**Access popup** shows a message or a coupon after a short scroll.

## Server and Discord

**Minecraft server status** shows how many players are online. It needs a server address.

**Discord card** controls how your store link looks when someone pastes it in Discord. It works with any theme and on every plan.

The card has a **Banner**, a **Title**, and **Text**. The title defaults to the store name, and the text defaults to the store description. **Button text** defaults to **View store**, and you can add an emoji. Leave **Accent color** empty to follow the store color.

You can add up to four sections. Each section has a title, text, and an optional button with an emoji and an `https` link. You can reorder the sections. Links in the **Footer** work.

<Tip>
  Discord can take up to 30 minutes to show changes on links that were already shared.
</Tip>
