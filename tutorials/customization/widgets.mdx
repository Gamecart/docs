## Discord card

When someone pastes your store link in Discord, Discord can show it as a rich card. The card works with any theme and on every plan. You set it up from the store's Discord card settings.

The card can include:

- A banner
- A title
- Text or a description
- A store button
- Up to four sections with buttons
- A footer
