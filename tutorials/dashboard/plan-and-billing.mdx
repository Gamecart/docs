Open the **Plan** page for your store in the dashboard to choose a Gamecart plan and pay for it.

Buyer checkout is separate. The methods buyers use in your shop are [payment gateways](/tutorials/store-setup/payment-gateways), under **Settings > Gateways**.

## Card

You can pay by card. The plan turns on when the card payment succeeds. Card billing is a subscription, so it renews on its own.

## Boleto

Boleto is offered when the plan price is in the Brazil region. Elsewhere, plan checkout is card only.

The plan changes only after Stripe confirms the Boleto payment. While that Boleto can still be paid, you can't start a second checkout.

On a renewal, if the Boleto is still payable when the period ends, the current plan stays active for 7 days past the period end.

## Pix

Pix pays for one period in advance. It does not renew on its own.

If you pay for the same tier again, the new period is added after the current one. A different tier waits until the prepaid period ends.

About 7 days before the prepaid period ends, Gamecart sends a reminder by email, in your inbox, and by push notification. The **Plan** page shows renewal options for that plan. When you extend it, you can choose 1 month or 1 year.

## Pending payment

When a Boleto or Pix payment is still outstanding, the **Plan** page shows a pending payment banner.
