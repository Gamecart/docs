Open the **Plan** page for your store in the dashboard to choose a Gamecart plan and pay for it.

Buyer checkout is separate. The methods buyers use in your shop are [payment gateways](/tutorials/store-setup/payment-gateways), under **Settings > Gateways**.

## Billing period

Pro and Enterprise can be billed monthly, quarterly, or yearly. On the **Plan** page, the **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, and **Yearly** tabs each show a **% OFF** badge. Quarterly saves about 10% compared with paying monthly. Yearly saves about 25%.

## Card

You can pay by card in any region and for any billing period. The plan turns on when the card payment succeeds. Card billing is a subscription, so it renews on its own.

## Boleto

Boleto is offered when the plan price is in the Brazil region. It works for monthly, quarterly, and yearly billing. Elsewhere, plan checkout is card only.

The plan changes only after Stripe confirms the Boleto payment. Pay the Boleto within 3 days. While that Boleto can still be paid, you can't start a second checkout.

On a renewal, if the Boleto is still payable when the period ends, the current plan stays active for 7 days past the period end.

A subscription paid with Boleto can't switch plan or billing period. Pay by card to change either one.

## Pix

Pix is available on Brazil-region prices, and only when the store has no card or Boleto subscription. It pays for one period in advance and does not renew on its own.

The plan cards offer **Pay 1 month with Pix**, **Pay 3 months with Pix**, and **Pay 1 year with Pix**. The Pix code is valid for 1 hour.

If you pay for the same tier again, the new period is added after the current one. A different tier waits until the prepaid period ends.

We'll email you a reminder before the period ends.

Open **Extend plan** or **Renew plan** to add time. Under **How long to extend**, choose **1 month**, **3 months**, or **1 year**. **1 year** is selected by default. The longer options show the monthly equivalent. Confirm with **Pay with Pix**.

## Pending payment

When a Boleto or Pix payment is still outstanding, the **Plan** page shows a pending payment banner.
