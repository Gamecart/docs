The dashboard can send push notifications for new sales, disputes, refunds, and new logins. Push works per device: turning it on for your phone doesn't turn it on for your laptop.

Everything happens on the **App** page. To open it, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and select **App**.

## Install the app

Installing is optional on a computer or Android, but required on iPhone and iPad.

**Desktop or Android**

1. On the **App** page, click **Install app**. If the button is hidden, use your browser menu to install the site.
2. Confirm the installation.
3. Open Gamecart from the new app icon.

**iPhone or iPad**

Safari only allows push notifications for apps on the Home Screen.

1. Open the dashboard in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Gamecart from the new Home Screen icon.

## Turn on notifications

1. On the **App** page, find **Notifications on this device**.
2. Click **Enable notifications**.
3. Allow notifications when the browser asks.

When it works, the page shows **Notifications enabled on this device**.

## When notifications don't turn on

If the page shows **Notifications are blocked** or **Notifications could not be updated**, the cause is almost always the browser or the device:

- If you blocked notifications for the site, the browser won't ask again. Allow them in the browser's site settings for the dashboard.
- Check the system settings on Windows, macOS, Android, or iOS. The browser, or the installed Gamecart app, must be allowed to show notifications.
- Most browsers turn push off in private windows. Use a regular window.
- Some privacy-focused browsers and extensions turn off push messaging by default. Allow it in the browser settings, or use another browser.
- On iPhone and iPad, push only works in the app on the Home Screen, not in a Safari tab. [Install the app](#install-the-app) and turn on notifications from there.

## Turn off push on a device

On that device, open the **App** page and click **Disable on this device**. Your other devices keep receiving notifications.

Email alerts for sales, disputes, and refunds are a separate setting for the store owner, in **Settings > Notifications**.
