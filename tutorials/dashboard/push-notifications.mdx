---
title: "Push notifications"
description: "Get dashboard alerts on your phone or computer, and fix the usual setup problems"
---

The dashboard can send push notifications to your device for new sales, disputes, refunds, and new logins. Push is set up per device: turning it on for your phone does not turn it on for your laptop.

## Turn on push notifications

1. In the dashboard, open your account menu and select **Profile**.
2. Open the **Notifications** tab.
3. Under **Push on this device**, click **Enable notifications**.
4. When your browser asks, allow notifications.

You can also do this from the **App** page in the sidebar.

On the same tab, the **Push** column controls which notifications reach your devices. Security, plan and Gamecart messages are always sent, so their switches are locked.

## iPhone and iPad

Safari only allows push notifications for apps added to the Home Screen.

1. Open the dashboard in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Gamecart from the new Home Screen icon.
4. Turn on notifications from there, following the steps above.

In a regular Safari tab, the dashboard asks you to add it to the Home Screen first.

## When notifications can't be set up

If the dashboard shows **Notifications couldn't be set up on this device**, the cause is almost always the browser or the device. Check these, then click **Enable notifications** again:

- **Notifications are blocked for the site.** Open your browser's site settings for the dashboard and allow notifications. If you blocked them earlier, the browser won't ask again until you change this setting.
- **Notifications are off at the system level.** On Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS, check that your browser, or the installed Gamecart app, may show notifications.
- **You're in a private or incognito window.** Most browsers disable push there. Use a regular window.
- **Your browser blocks push services.** Some privacy-focused browsers and extensions turn off push messaging by default. Allow it in the browser settings, or use a different browser.
- **On iPhone or iPad, you're not using the Home Screen app.** Follow the steps in [iPhone and iPad](#iphone-and-ipad).

## Turn off push on a device

Open **Profile > Notifications** on that device and click **Disable on this device**. Your other devices keep receiving notifications.
