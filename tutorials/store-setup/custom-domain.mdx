Your storefront always has a `gamecart.gg` address. With a custom domain, buyers reach it at a hostname you own, such as `store.example.com`. Gamecart takes care of routing and HTTPS once the DNS records are in place.

You need the Pro plan and access to your domain's DNS settings.

## Add the domain in the dashboard

1. Open **Settings > Domain**.
2. Turn on **Custom domain**.
3. Enter the hostname, such as `store.example.com`. Leave out `https://`, paths, and trailing slashes.
4. Click **Save**.

## Create the DNS records

After you save, the dashboard shows two records. Create both at your DNS provider, copying the values with the copy buttons.

| Type | Name | Value | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CNAME` | Your hostname | `dns.gamecart.gg` | Sends storefront traffic to Gamecart |
| `TXT` | `_gamecart.<your hostname>` | The token shown in the dashboard | Proves you own the domain |

<Frame caption="Copy both records exactly as the dashboard shows them.">
  <img
    src="/images/tutorials/custom-domain/dns-records.png"
    alt="DNS records table with the CNAME and TXT records and their copy buttons highlighted"
  />
</Frame>

If your DNS provider can proxy traffic, as Cloudflare does, set both records to **DNS only**.

<Tip>
  Providers format record names differently. Some expect the full name, such as `_gamecart.store.example.com`. Others add the domain for you, so you type only `_gamecart.store`. Check the saved record before you move on.
</Tip>

To use the bare domain, such as `example.com`, your DNS provider must support CNAME flattening or an ALIAS record. If it doesn't, use a subdomain such as `store.example.com`.

## Wait for activation

The dashboard checks the records every 15 seconds while the page is open, and Gamecart keeps checking in the background. The **Status** column turns **Confirmed** for each record as it is found.

<Frame caption="While the domain is pending, the records stay on screen with their status.">
  <img
    src="/images/tutorials/custom-domain/domain-pending.png"
    alt="Domain settings with the Pending status, the Re-check button, and the DNS records highlighted"
  />
</Frame>

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Gamecart hasn't found the `TXT` record yet. |
| **Validating** | Ownership is confirmed, and Gamecart is setting up routing and HTTPS. |
| **Active** | The storefront answers at your domain over HTTPS. The dashboard shows the date it went live. |
| **Failed** | Setup stopped. Fix the records, then click **Re-check**. |

DNS changes can take a while to spread. Click **Re-check** to check again without waiting.

## If the domain doesn't activate

- Look up the saved records and make sure the provider didn't add your domain twice, as in `_gamecart.store.example.com.example.com`.
- Set both records to **DNS only** if your provider proxies traffic.
- Remove old `A`, `AAAA`, or `CNAME` records on the same hostname.
- For a bare domain, confirm the provider supports CNAME flattening or ALIAS records.

<Warning>
  Don't delete DNS records you don't recognize until you know what they do. They may serve another site, your email, or another integration.
</Warning>

## Remove the domain

Turn off **Custom domain** and click **Save**, then confirm with **Remove domain**. The storefront stays available at its `gamecart.gg` address. To use the domain again later, you add it and create the records again.
