A Gamecart account can own up to 10 stores. Each store has its own products, payments, and server connections.

## Create an account

1. Go to [dash.gamecart.gg](https://dash.gamecart.gg) and click **Sign up**.
2. Enter your email and click **Continue**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code that Gamecart sent to your email.
4. Fill in your name and password, then click **Create Account**.
5. Sign in with the new account.

Every sign-in asks for a code sent to your email. Check **Trust this browser for 30 days** to skip the code on that browser.

## Store setup

After you sign in for the first time, the dashboard opens store setup. The form is a 3-step accordion. The header shows **Step 1 of 3**, **Step 2 of 3**, or **Step 3 of 3**.

**Back** returns to the previous step. **Continue** opens the next one. The last step uses **Create Store**.

## Store type

The first step is **Store type**: "Choose the game your server runs."

1. Under **Game**, select **Minecraft**, **Hytale**, **FiveM**, or **Other / Generic**. No card is selected when the step opens.
2. Click **Continue**.

The game decides which delivery methods and command defaults the store offers. You can't change the game after the store is created. **Settings > General** shows it, and you can't edit it there.

## Store details

The second step is **Store details**: "Choose a name and configure your store address."

1. **Store name**: the name buyers see. Use 3 to 32 characters. Typing the name fills in the subdomain.
2. **Subdomain**: the storefront address, such as `blockhaven.gamecart.shop`.
3. **Store color**: **Violet**, **Blue**, **Orange**, **Emerald**, **Red**, or **Gold**. **Violet** is selected by default. **Custom** opens a color picker, and a secondary shade is derived automatically. The hint says "You can change it later."
4. Click **Continue**.

A subdomain uses 3 to 32 lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens, and it must start with a letter or a number. The availability check shows **Subdomain is available!** or **This subdomain is already taken.** You can [use your own domain](/tutorials/store-setup/custom-domain).

The preset main colors are:

| Preset | Main color |
| --- | --- |
| Violet (default) | `#a855f7` |
| Blue | `#3c7cfb` |
| Orange | `#f97316` |
| Emerald | `#10b981` |
| Red | `#ef4444` |
| Gold | `#f5a524` |

To change the colors later, open **Your store > Customization > Visual identity**. On the **Colors** card, edit **Main Color** and **Secondary Color**, then click **Save changes**.

## Region

The third step is **Region**: "Set the currency and time zone for your store."

The step shows this notice: "The currency can't be changed after the store is created."

1. **Default currency**: **USD**, **BRL**, **EUR**, or **GBP**. The selected currency is the one suggested from your language, or **USD** when there is no suggestion.
2. **Timezone**: a searchable list. Each entry looks like `(UTC±HH:MM) Area/City`. Your browser's timezone is selected. The hint says "You can change it later in Settings."
3. Click **Create Store**.

<Warning>
  Some regional gateways only work in stores that sell in a specific currency, so check [Payment gateways](/tutorials/store-setup/payment-gateways) before you pick one.
</Warning>

**Settings > General** shows the currency, and you can't edit it there. Dates in your store use the **Timezone** you set on that page. The dashboard keeps using your browser's timezone.

## Next steps

After the store is created, a toast says **Store created.** The dashboard opens with a guided tour.

The store picker in the top left switches between your stores.
