---
title: "Stripe"
description: "Connect Stripe to a Gamecart store"
---

Stripe takes cards and wallets in any store currency. Gamecart only needs your Stripe secret key. After you save it, Gamecart creates the webhook on your Stripe account, so you don't copy a webhook secret.

## Get the secret key in Stripe

1. Open the [Stripe Dashboard](https://dashboard.stripe.com).
2. Go to **Developers > API keys**.
3. Copy the **Secret key**. It starts with `sk_test_` in test mode or `sk_live_` in live mode.

Use a test key while you try the integration. Don't use the publishable key (`pk_`).

## Add it in Gamecart

1. Open **Settings > Gateways** and click **Add gateway**.
2. Click **Add** on **Stripe**.
3. Paste the key in **Stripe secret key**.
4. Click **Save Configuration**.

5. Turn on the Stripe switch in the gateway list when you are ready to take payments.

If you later replace the secret key, Gamecart creates a new webhook for the new key. See [Payment gateways](/tutorials/store-setup/payment-gateways) to reorder or remove the gateway.
