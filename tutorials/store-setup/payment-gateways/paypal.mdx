---

Gamecart sends buyers to PayPal Payments Standard. You only need the email address of the PayPal account that receives the money. Gamecart doesn't use PayPal REST client IDs or a webhook secret.

## Get the receiver email

Use the email address of the PayPal account that should receive store payments. PayPal calls it the `business` value on the checkout form.

To test first, use the email of a PayPal sandbox account.

## Add it in Gamecart

1. Open **Settings > Gateways** and click **Add gateway**.
2. Click **Add** on **PayPal**.
3. Paste the address in **Receiver email**.
4. Turn on **Sandbox mode** only if the email belongs to a PayPal sandbox account.
5. Click **Save Configuration**.

6. Turn on the PayPal switch in the gateway list when you are ready to take payments.
