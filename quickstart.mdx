---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Go from a new account to a store that takes real orders"
---

These five steps take you from a new account to your first order. Each one links to a tutorial with the details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your store">
    Sign up at [dash.gamecart.gg](https://dash.gamecart.gg), pick your game, and choose the store name, currency, and subdomain. You can't change the game or the currency later. [Create your store](/tutorials/store-setup/create-store).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add products">
    Create a category, then the products buyers can purchase, with price and description. [Add products](/tutorials/store-setup/products).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up payments">
    Add at least one gateway in **Settings > Gateways** and turn it on. Pix and Mercado Pago need a BRL store. [Payment gateways](/tutorials/store-setup/payment-gateways).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your game server">
    Connect the server that receives purchases and add delivery commands to your products. [Server delivery](/tutorials/server-delivery/overview).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test an order">
    Open your storefront, buy a product, and check that the payment is approved in **Sales > Orders** and the command ran on the server. [Test server delivery](/tutorials/server-delivery/troubleshooting).
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the store works, you can [use your own domain](/tutorials/store-setup/custom-domain) instead of the `gamecart.gg` address.

<Tip>
  Stuck on a step? Ask in the [Gamecart community](https://discord.gg/gamecart).
</Tip>
