This guide takes you from a new Gamecart account to a storefront that can receive real orders. Use the linked tutorials when you need more detail about a step.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you need:

- A Gamecart account.
- Access to the dashboard at [dash.gamecart.gg](https://dash.gamecart.gg).
- A game server or delivery workflow ready to receive purchase commands.
- Payment provider credentials for the gateway you plan to enable.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your store">
    Sign in to the dashboard and create a store with the name, subdomain, and currency you want to use for sales.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first product">
    Set the product price, delivery details, and the storefront information players need before they buy. If the product uses game delivery, [configure its delivery commands](/tutorials/server-delivery/configure-commands).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure payments">
    Enable a payment gateway that supports your region and store currency. Gamecart controls gateway availability and checkout routing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect delivery">
    Follow the [server delivery overview](/tutorials/server-delivery/overview) to choose and configure the Gamecart server integration for your game server.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and test">
    Open the storefront and place a controlled test order. [Check the delivery result](/tutorials/server-delivery/troubleshooting), then confirm the payment status and order details in the dashboard. To use your own hostname, [set up a custom domain](/tutorials/store-setup/custom-domain).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Need help while setting up? Join the [Gamecart community](https://discord.gg/gamecart).
</Tip>
